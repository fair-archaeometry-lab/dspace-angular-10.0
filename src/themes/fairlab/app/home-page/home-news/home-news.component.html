Platform built for FAIR Future of Archaeometry

FAIR Archaeometry Lab is a digital portal designed to support the management of laboratory workflows in a way that is truly FAIR—Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable.

Portal Features

Discover service providers, devices, and analysis methods tailored to your specific sampling needs.

Register, manage, and share your digital resources using persistent identifiers and automated workflows

Standardise your metadata descriptions with community-driven schemas and controlled vocabularies.

Exchange data with global repositories and aggregators via standard OAI-PMH harvesting
